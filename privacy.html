Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who uses the app

Avatar Studio is a private, self-hosted tool used by its owner only. It has no other users and collects no data about visitors or third parties.

2. Data the app handles

  • Connection data. When the owner connects a social media account (for example TikTok), the app receives access tokens and basic profile information (display name, avatar, account ID) for that account. These are stored on the owner's own server and are used only to upload the owner's videos to that account.
  • Content. Pictures, voice-overs and videos made by the owner are stored on the owner's own server.

3. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any data. Content is sent only to the platform the owner chooses to publish to (for example, a video uploaded to the owner's TikTok inbox) and to the AI services the owner uses to create content.

4. Retention and deletion

Access tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account in the app's settings or revokes access in the platform's settings; disconnecting deletes them from the server. Content is kept until the owner deletes it.
